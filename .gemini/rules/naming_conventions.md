# Blog Post Naming & Language Conventions

* **Always use short, concise titles and filenames**:
  * Keep post titles strictly focused on the core concept (e.g., `TCP Head-of-Line Blocking`, `TCP_NOTSENT_LOWAT`, `Socket Buffer Tuning`, `Cloud HFT with DPDK`).
  * Never append long subtitles or explanations to titles (e.g., avoid `Title: Why Strict FIFO Breaks...`).
  * Slugs and filenames must be compact (e.g., `2026-11-08-tcp-hol-blocking.markdown`).

* **Strict English Language Policy**:
  * Write entirely in clean English.
  * Never add Chinese explanations, translations, or parenthetical annotations (e.g., no `(队头阻塞)` or `(缓冲区膨胀)`) unless the user explicitly asks to add Chinese for a specific term.
